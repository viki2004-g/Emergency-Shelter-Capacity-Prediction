{# ── Assumptions Panel ───────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Model Assumptions & Methodology — Transparent Disclosure
Formula:
Future Occupancy = Current Occupancy + (Arrival Rate × Hours)
Arrival Rate = (newest_occ − oldest_occ) / elapsed_hours
Data window: Last 10 occupancy records per shelter
Method: Simple linear rate extrapolation
Seasonality: Not modelled
ML: Not used — pure arithmetic
What this means: If arrivals were averaging 5 people/hour in the last few recorded updates, and current occupancy is 100, then in 6 hours this model estimates occupancy at 130. It does not know about traffic, weather, events, or time-of-day patterns. A negative arrival rate (people leaving) similarly projects a lower future load — but the shelter could refill suddenly.
